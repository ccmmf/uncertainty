MAGiC uncertainty training

Authors

Akash B V

David LeBauer

This is the uncertainty component of the MAGiC system. It runs SIPNET at design points with the same meteorology, initial conditions, management events, and parameter priors as the ensemble workflow, and reports which of them the modeled soil carbon and nitrous oxide flux are most sensitive to.

Two analyses are run:

  • A local (one-at-a-time) analysis moves each parameter in turn across its prior and reports each parameter’s share of the parameter variance.
  • A global (Sobol) analysis varies parameters, initial conditions, meteorology, and management together and reports the share of output variance from each.

Each notebook is a self-study demo to work through beforehand, then discuss in the live session.

Commands

Command What it does
prepare Stages the ensemble inputs into run_dir and builds the settings of both analyses.
local-sensitivity Runs the one-at-a-time analysis, computes each parameter’s share of variance at each design point, and draws the figures.
global-sensitivity Runs the Sobol analysis, computes the indices with bootstrap intervals at each design point, and draws the figures.
